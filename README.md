## 👋 Overview
Welcome to my Power BI project repository! This portfolio showcases the interactive business intelligence reports and semantic models I designed and developed during my **Data Technician Bootcamp** (aligned with Microsoft PL-300 standards). Using enterprise-grade retail, sales, and operational data, these dashboards demonstrate how to transform raw data sources into executive-ready insights.

---

## 🛠️ Core Technical Skills & Capabilities

* 🧹 **Data Transformation & Cleaning (Power Query):** Importing, shaping, and transforming data sources to establish clean, optimized semantic models.
* 🧮 **DAX Calculations:** Building custom calculated columns and complex measures to evaluate performance metrics, profit margins, and sales variances.
* 🎛️ **Interactive Reporting & Slicers:** Implementing dynamic filtering (Year, Region, Segment) to empower users with self-service exploratory analysis.
* 🎨 **Advanced Data Visualizations:**
  * 📊 **Bar & Column Charts:** Tracking monthly sales performance against targets and volume breakdowns by category.
  * 📉 **Line Charts & Trend Analysis:** Visualizing longitudinal growth trajectories for revenue and profitability over time.
  * 🗺️ **Geographic Maps:** Mapping regional profitability and market performance across international territories.
  * 🧩 **Treemaps & Matrix Tables:** Highlighting top-performing products and structured financial summaries.
* 📖 **Data Storytelling:** Translating commercial retail data into cohesive, professional narratives that drive strategic business decisions.
